# Year Up, a year of training and internship for young adults.
# A lottery study with seven years of wage records. Two things make it a good test.
# Seats are limited, and the earnings gain was measured over everyone offered a seat.
# Fein and Dastrup, Benefits that Last, OPRE Report 2022-77. Public domain.

product: Year Up
built for: young adults 18 to 24 who applied, were found eligible, and won a seat by lottery

years: Enrolled 2013 to 2014, followed for seven years
outcome: higher earnings
